Glasshouse Budget support

Help with the app, and how to reach us.

Glasshouse Budget gives each of you a personal budget, keeps your shared costs split the way you agree, and keeps a running tally of who owes whom. It also tracks savings funds, net worth and take-home pay.

Getting started together

One of you creates an account and starts a budget. In Settings, tap Invite your partner to get a code. Your partner creates their own account, chooses Join with a code and types it in. You'll both see the same budget on your own phones.

Connecting a bank

Open Banks (from the ⋯ menu or Settings) and choose Connect. You sign in with your bank through Plaid; the app never sees your bank password. Purchases then file themselves, and you can change any category or split afterwards.

Take-home pay and taxes

On Personal, tap the income card to enter your salary, state and filing status, and any side-business profit. The app estimates federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare, and self-employment tax. These are estimates for budgeting, not tax advice.

Deleting your account

Open Settings and choose Delete account. This removes your sign-in, disconnects your banks, and deletes your budget if nobody else is in it.
