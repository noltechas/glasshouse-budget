Privacy Policy

Glasshouse Budget · Effective November 10, 2026

Glasshouse Budget is a budgeting app for couples, made by Chas Nolte. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: your information is used only to run your budget. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to track you.

What the app collects

  • Your account: the email address and password you sign up with. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider.
  • Your budget: what you enter or import, such as the names you give yourself and your partner, purchases, categories and budgets, funds, income, accounts, home and loan details, and salary and tax settings.
  • Bank information, if you connect a bank: through our provider Plaid, the app receives the names, types, last four digits and balances of the accounts you choose, and their transactions. The app never sees your bank username or password; you enter those with Plaid directly.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or browsing activity, and it contains no advertising or analytics software.

How it's used

Only to provide the app: to keep your budget in step between your devices and your partner's, to bring in purchases and balances from connected banks, and to estimate taxes and projections. Tax figures are estimates for budgeting and are not tax advice.

Who can see it

  • Your partner: anyone you invite to your budget with an invite code sees and can change the shared budget.
  • Service providers: your data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in, hosted in the United States). Bank connections run through Plaid, which handles bank data under its own End User Privacy Policy. Bank access tokens are kept encrypted on the server and never sent to your device.
  • Nobody else: your information is not sold, rented or shared for marketing. It may be disclosed only if required by law.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Each budget is kept separate by access rules in the database, so only its members can read it.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information is kept for as long as you have an account. To delete it, open Settings in the app and choose Delete account. This removes your sign-in, disconnects your banks at Plaid, and deletes your budget if nobody else is in it. If your partner is still in the budget, they keep the shared budget. Deleted information may remain in routine backups for a short time before it is overwritten. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
